Key Experiments

  • Trigger wave: Default settings (each cell is coupled only to its nearest neighbours). Click "Stimulate Centre" — two waves travel outward at a constant 1 cell/step without losing amplitude. Every cell fires exactly once and is followed by a refractory wake (red → blue in the kymograph).
  • Wave collision: Click "Both Ends" — the waves meet and annihilate, because each runs into the refractory cells left behind by the other.
  • Role of the refractory period: Lower τref from 4 to 2 — cells recover while the messenger released by their neighbours is still above threshold, so they fire again and the stimulus site becomes a pacemaker emitting a train of waves (also with τref = 0). Raise τsig to 8 and re-excitation already appears at τref = 5: refractoriness must outlast the signal for a single, one-way wave.
  • Threshold for propagation: With nearest-neighbour coupling a firing cell gives its neighbour an input of D·λ = 0.30 × 0.65 = 0.195. Raise θ to 0.20 (just above it) and the wave no longer propagates; equivalently, lower D to 0.25 at θ = 0.18. With coupling range R, a wave moving 1 cell/step propagates if θ < D·λ·(1 − λR)/(1 − λ), the input from R firing cells behind the front (valid when τpulse ≥ R, so that all of them are still firing).
  • Starting vs. sustaining a wave (long-range coupling): Set coupling range = 8, τpulse = 8, τref = 15. The wave is now faster (2–3 cells/step), because each cell feels the whole active region behind the front. A click excites 3 cells, which give the next cell an input of D·(λ + λ² + λ³) ≈ 0.40; a running wave has 8 firing cells behind its front, giving D·λ·(1 − λ8)/(1 − λ) ≈ 0.54. Raise θ to 0.45 and click "Stimulate Left" — no wave starts. Now set θ back to 0.30, click "Stimulate Left" and, while the wave is travelling, drag θ up to 0.45: the running wave keeps going (1 cell/step), because its front is driven by the many active cells behind it. Starting a trigger wave is harder than sustaining one.

The status bar classifies the current parameters by running hidden test waves from the left end: one started by a normal 3-cell click, and one that is already running.
